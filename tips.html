A little clever goes a long way!

Simple tricks, handy ideas, and everyday shortcuts that save time, money, and a little sanity.

🧊 Freeze leftover coffee

Pour leftover coffee into an ice cube tray. Freeze it and use the cubes in iced coffee later. No more watered-down coffee!

🍋 Freshen up your microwave

Put a microwave-safe bowl with water and a few lemon slices inside. Heat for a couple of minutes, let it sit, then wipe away loosened splatters.

🧦 Keep sheet sets together

Fold your fitted sheet, flat sheet, and pillowcases together, then tuck the bundle inside one pillowcase. Your linen closet just got organized!

🛒 Shop your pantry first

Before grocery shopping, check what you already have. Build a few meals around those ingredients and you may save yourself a surprising amount.

📱 Take a picture before you unplug

Before disconnecting a complicated set of cords, snap a photo. You'll have a handy reference when it's time to plug everything back in.

🌿 Make herbs last longer

Trim the ends of fresh herbs, stand them in a small glass with a little water, and loosely cover them. Store in the refrigerator when appropriate for the herb.
